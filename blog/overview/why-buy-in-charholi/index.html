Why Charholi is on every Pune home buyer’s radar

By Tanish Group · Published 25 September 2026 · Updated 25 September 2026

Charholi sits in the Pimpri-Chinchwad Municipal Corporation (PCMC) belt of Pune, close to Ajinkya D.Y. Patil University. It is one of the parts of the city that is developing fastest, with new infrastructure and landmark projects coming up in the surrounding area.

What buyers look for in a growing corridor

A growing corridor tends to bring three things together: better road and public connectivity, more day-to-day services, and a steady stream of new residential communities. Together they usually make a neighbourhood easier to live in as time passes.

Where Tanish Urbania fits

Tanish Urbania is planned as a contemporary residential community in this corridor, with 12 residential wings, landscaped open areas and lifestyle amenities. The aim is a home that offers comfort and connectivity today and long-term value later.

Before you decide

Visit the area at different times of the day, check the routes you would take to work or school, and ask the sales team for the current list of nearby facilities. Seeing the neighbourhood yourself tells you more than any brochure.
