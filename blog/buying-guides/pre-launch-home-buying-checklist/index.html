A checklist for buying a home before the launch

By Tanish Group · Published 25 September 2026 · Updated 25 September 2026

Buying early can give you access to good options, but it also asks you to check a few things carefully. Use this list for any upcoming project, including ours.

Six checks

  • RERA registration. Confirm the project’s registration number and details on the MahaRERA website.
  • Developer track record. Look at earlier projects and how they were delivered.
  • Approved plans. Ask to see the sanctioned plans and the carpet area of your unit.
  • Payment schedule. Get the full schedule in writing, with all charges listed.
  • Possession timeline. Note the committed date in the agreement.
  • Total cost. Ask for the all-inclusive price, including taxes, registration and maintenance deposits.

Take your time

A good developer will answer these questions openly. Tanish Urbania is launching soon; register your interest and our team will share the details as they are confirmed.
