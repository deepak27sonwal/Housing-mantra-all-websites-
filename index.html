Project Overview

Simple, open and built around community

Tanish Urbania is a contemporary residential community in Charholi, Pune, designed for a rapidly developing urban corridor. With strong infrastructure growth and landmark projects emerging nearby, it offers comfort, connectivity and long-term value.

Twelve residential wings are positioned to ensure ample open space, daylight and ventilation. The architecture is defined by clean geometry, vertical accents and balanced proportions, with generous balconies and a subtle play of colour.

Architecture by YW Architects
