Charholi lies in the Pimpri-Chinchwad (PCMC) part of Pune, close to Ajeenkya D.Y. Patil University in Lohegaon. The area is served by D.Y. Patil University Road, Kaljewadi Road and the Pune-Alandi Road, and it is close to Alandi, Moshi and Bhosari.
