2 BHK or 3 BHK: how to choose the right home

By Tanish Group · Published 25 September 2026 · Updated 25 September 2026

Choosing between a 2 BHK and a 3 BHK is mostly a question of how you live today and how you expect to live in five to ten years.

The 2 BHK case

At Tanish Urbania, 2 BHK homes are approximately 720 to 770 sq ft. This size suits couples, small families and first-time buyers who want an efficient layout, a lower purchase price and lower running costs.

The 3 BHK case

3 BHK homes are approximately 920 to 980 sq ft. The extra bedroom works as a children’s room, a guest room or a home office, which is why many families prefer it if they plan to work from home or expect the household to grow.

Questions to ask yourself

  • Will someone work from home most days?
  • Do parents or guests stay with you often?
  • How long do you plan to live here?
  • What monthly EMI is comfortable for you?
